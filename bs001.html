使用说明K–23–5复习

怎么学

本学习册不是某一所美国学校的原版教材，而是按照美国小学 ELA（English Language Arts）的常见学习方向整理的原创英语课文。美国小学课程通常强调阅读、写作、讨论、词汇与知识积累；不同州、学区和学校使用的教材并不完全相同。

为了避免直接复制仍受版权保护的教材，本册采用原创短文，并加入少量美国小学常见主题与公共领域经典素材的学习方式。建议每天 20–30 分钟：①朗读 ②理解 ③词汇 ④练习 ⑤背诵。

K–2 基础阅读

Grade 1生活

Lesson 1 · My School Morning

我的学校早晨

① 英文课文

Every morning, I wake up when the sun comes through my window. I get dressed and eat breakfast with my family. Then I put my books in my backpack. My mom says, “Have a good day!” I walk to school with my friend Ben. We talk about our plans for the day. When we see our teacher, we say, “Good morning!” I like school because every day I learn something new.

② 中文翻译

每天早上，太阳从窗户照进来时，我就醒了。我穿好衣服，和家人一起吃早餐。然后把书放进书包。妈妈对我说：“祝你今天愉快！”我和朋友 Ben 一起走去学校。我们聊着今天的计划。看到老师时，我们说：“早上好！”我喜欢学校，因为每天我都会学到新的东西。

③ 核心词汇

wake up醒来
window窗户
backpack书包
plan计划
teacher老师
something new新东西

④ 语法

一般现在时：描述每天发生的事情。
Every morning, I wake up... / I walk to school.
like + 名词：I like school. = 我喜欢学校。

⑤ 练习

1. Where does the writer go every morning?
2. Who walks to school with the writer?
3. 把 “I learn something new every day.” 翻译成中文。
参考：1. School. 2. Ben. 3. 我每天都会学到新的东西。

⑥ 背诵

先背这 3 句：Every morning, I wake up when the sun comes through my window.
I walk to school with my friend Ben.
I like school because every day I learn something new.
Grade 2自然

Lesson 2 · A Little Seed

一粒小种子

① 英文课文

A little seed is sleeping under the soil. It is dark and quiet there. After the rain, the seed begins to grow. First, a small root goes down. Then a green stem comes up. The plant needs sunlight and water. Days later, two tiny leaves open to the sky. The plant is still small, but it is getting stronger every day.

② 中文翻译

一粒小种子正在土壤下面沉睡。那里又黑又安静。下雨之后，种子开始生长。首先，一条小小的根向下生长。然后，一根绿色的茎向上长出来。植物需要阳光和水。几天后，两片小小的叶子向天空展开。植物仍然很小，但它每天都在变得更强壮。

③ 核心词汇

seed种子
soil土壤
root根
stem茎
sunlight阳光
stronger更强壮

④ 语法

begin to + 动词：begins to grow = 开始生长。
need + 名词：The plant needs sunlight and water.

⑤ 练习

1. Where is the seed?
2. What comes down first, the root or the stem?
3. What does the plant need?
参考：1. Under the soil. 2. The root. 3. Sunlight and water.

⑥ 背诵

The plant needs sunlight and water. Days later, two tiny leaves open to the sky.

Grades 3–5 进阶阅读

Grade 3科学

Lesson 3 · Why Do We Have Seasons?

我们为什么会有四季？

① 英文课文

Earth has four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter. The seasons happen because Earth travels around the Sun and its axis is tilted. When one part of Earth is tilted toward the Sun, that part gets more direct sunlight and has warmer weather. When it is tilted away from the Sun, the weather is usually colder. The seasons change as Earth continues its journey around the Sun.

② 中文翻译

地球有四个季节：春、夏、秋、冬。四季的变化是因为地球绕太阳运行，同时地球的自转轴是倾斜的。当地球的一部分朝向太阳倾斜时，它会得到更多直射阳光，因此天气更温暖。当它背向太阳时，天气通常更冷。随着地球继续绕太阳运行，季节也随之变化。

③ 核心词汇

season季节
Earth地球
axis轴
tilted倾斜的
direct直接的
journey旅程

④ 语法

because 引导原因：The seasons happen because Earth travels around the Sun.
when 引导时间：When one part of Earth is tilted toward the Sun...

⑤ 练习

判断正误：
A. Earth has three seasons. □
B. Earth's axis is tilted. □
C. Direct sunlight can make weather warmer. □
参考：A False · B True · C True

⑥ 背诵

Earth has four seasons: spring, summer, fall, and winter. The seasons change as Earth continues its journey around the Sun.
Grade 4故事

Lesson 4 · The Lost Map

丢失的地图

① 英文课文

Mia found an old map inside a library book. The map showed a small lake, a bridge, and a tall oak tree. She wanted to know where the map came from, so she asked the librarian for help. Together, they searched the library records. They discovered that the map had been drawn by a student many years ago. The student had made it for a school project about the town. Mia did not find buried treasure, but she found something better: a story from the past.

② 中文翻译

Mia 在一本图书馆的书里发现了一张旧地图。地图上画着一个小湖、一座桥和一棵高大的橡树。她想知道这张地图来自哪里，于是请图书管理员帮忙。他们一起查找图书馆的记录。他们发现，这张地图是许多年前一名学生画的。这名学生当时为了完成一个关于小镇的学校项目而制作了地图。Mia 没有找到埋藏的宝藏，但她发现了更好的东西：一个来自过去的故事。

③ 核心词汇

map地图
library图书馆
librarian图书管理员
record记录
discover发现
treasure宝藏

④ 语法

want to + 动词：She wanted to know...
过去完成时的基础理解：had been drawn / had made 表示“在过去某个时间之前已经发生”。

⑤ 练习

1. Where did Mia find the map?
2. Who made the map?
3. What did Mia find instead of treasure?
参考：1. Inside a library book. 2. A student many years ago. 3. A story from the past.

⑥ 背诵

Mia did not find buried treasure, but she found something better: a story from the past.
Grade 5人物

Lesson 5 · A Question Can Start an Adventure

一个问题可以开启一场冒险

① 英文课文

When Noah visited the science museum, he noticed a model of a spacecraft. He wondered how astronauts could live in space for months. Instead of simply reading the answer, he wrote down three questions. How do they get water? How do they eat? How do they sleep? His teacher encouraged him to investigate. Noah read books, watched classroom demonstrations, and compared information from different sources. At the end of the week, he presented his findings to the class. He learned that asking a good question is often the first step toward discovering a good answer.

② 中文翻译

Noah 参观科学博物馆时，注意到一个宇宙飞船模型。他想知道宇航员怎样才能在太空中生活几个月。他没有只是直接阅读答案，而是写下了三个问题：他们怎样获得水？怎样吃饭？怎样睡觉？老师鼓励他进行调查。Noah 阅读书籍、观看课堂演示，并比较来自不同来源的信息。一周结束时，他向全班展示了自己的研究结果。他了解到：提出一个好问题，往往是找到好答案的第一步。

③ 核心词汇

spacecraft宇宙飞船
astronaut宇航员
wonder想知道
investigate调查、研究
source来源
finding研究发现

④ 语法

间接理解：He wondered how astronauts could live in space. = 他想知道宇航员如何在太空生活。
Instead of + 动词-ing：Instead of simply reading the answer...

⑤ 练习

用英文回答：
1. What did Noah see at the museum?
2. What did his teacher encourage him to do?
3. What did he learn?
参考：1. A model of a spacecraft. 2. Investigate. 3. A good question can be the first step toward a good answer.

⑥ 背诵

He learned that asking a good question is often the first step toward discovering a good answer.

综合复习

① 单词回忆

不看前文，写出下面单词的英文：

② 句型替换

I like school because every day I learn something new.
尝试替换：I like ______ because ______.

③ 英译中

A good question can be the first step toward discovering a good answer.

④ 中译英

我每天都会学到新的东西。
地球有四个季节。
她在一本图书馆的书里发现了一张旧地图。

⑤ 背诵方法

四遍法：
第 1 遍：看英文朗读。
第 2 遍：看中文，回想英文。
第 3 遍：遮住英文，独立说。
第 4 遍：不看任何内容，完整复述。
学习路线建议：Grade 1 → Grade 2 → Grade 3 → Grade 4 → Grade 5。后续可继续扩展为完整课程，每课加入听力脚本、跟读、口语问答、默写、错题记录和阶段测试。
