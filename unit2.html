💡 文化洞见 Key Insight
Always get agreements in writing — verbal promises don't hold up.
Typical lease terms: 12 months. Breaking early usually costs 1-2 months rent.
Security deposit = 押金, usually 1-2 months rent, returned if no damage.
Give 30-day written notice before moving out — or you lose your deposit.
Report repairs IN WRITING (text/email) so you have a record.
所有协议都要书面确认——口头承诺没有法律效力。典型租约期限：12个月。提前退租通常需要支付1-2个月租金。押金通常为1-2个月租金，无损坏则退还。搬出前需提前30天书面通知。维修要以书面形式（短信/邮件）报告，这样有记录。

"Paper or plastic?" = What kind of bag do you want?

Store loyalty cards give discounts — worth signing up for free.

💡 文化洞见 Key Insight
Checking account (支票账户) = for daily spending. Savings account (储蓄账户) = for saving.
ATM fees: using another bank's ATM usually costs $2-4. Use your own bank's ATM to avoid fees.
"Overdraft" = your account goes below zero. Banks charge $25-35 per overdraft. Avoid this.
Routing number (路由号码) = bank ID for transfers. Account number = your specific account.
Zelle, Venmo, and Cash App are popular for person-to-person transfers.
支票账户 = 日常消费用。储蓄账户 = 存钱用。ATM手续费：使用其他银行的ATM通常收费2-4美元。用自己银行的ATM可避免手续费。"Overdraft" = 账户余额为负。银行每次透支收费25-35美元，要避免。路由号码 = 银行标识，用于转账。账号 = 你的具体账户。Zelle、Venmo和Cash App是常用的个人转账工具。
